# TDCC primary + channel websites supplement for fund universe and NAV

Fund universe and daily NAVs are sourced from TDCC's Fund Information Observation Platform as the single primary source, supplemented by the three channels' own websites (Yuanta Securities, HSBC, Standard Chartered) to fill gaps TDCC misses. The universe is re-discovered automatically per channel every run and snapshotted to JSON; there is no manually maintained fund list.
